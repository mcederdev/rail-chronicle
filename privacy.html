Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version: Rail Chronicle has no accounts and I don't collect your personal data. Your progress is saved on your iPhone. The game is free, and it shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for an income boost. Those ads come from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show and measure them, after asking for your consent where the law requires it. Purchases of Tickets and the monthly pass are handled by Apple.

1. Who is responsible

Rail Chronicle is made and published by an independent developer, Magnus Cederberg, based in Sweden ("I", "me"). You can reach me at mcederdev@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Rail Chronicle app for iPhone and this website.

2. Data that stays on your iPhone

To work, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • your progress: Cash, upgrades, research, stations, scenery, eras, Concessions, Tickets, any running boost and your Museum collection, and
  • your settings: sound, volume and haptics.

This data is never sent to me. It is deleted when you delete the app. If you have device backups turned on, Apple may include it in your backup under Apple's own terms.

3. Data I collect

None. Rail Chronicle has no sign-in, no account, no analytics of its own and no servers of its own. I don't receive your name, email, location, contacts or anything else from the app. If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write, and I use that only to answer you.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rail Chronicle shows only rewarded ads, provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited. An ad plays only when you tap to watch one for a 2× income boost. There are no banner ads and no ads between screens. If you have the monthly pass, you get the boost without watching an ad.

To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, prevent fraud and measure how ads perform, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and process:

DataExamples
Device identifiersThe Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking. Otherwise an app-specific identifier (IDFV).
Approximate locationDerived from your IP address, for example the country or city. Not your precise GPS location.
Device and app informationDevice model, operating system version, language, app version.
Ad interactionsWhich ads were shown, whether you watched or tapped them.
DiagnosticsCrash and performance data from the ads SDK.

AdMob may fill an ad request through other ad networks it works with (mediation). If I add specific ad networks to the game this way, I will name them in this policy.

Google processes this data under its own policies. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent, tracking and personalised ads

Before any ad loads, the game shows Google's consent form where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. iOS may also ask whether Rail Chronicle may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the app cannot access your Advertising Identifier and you will see non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and change your consent choice with Privacy choices in the game's settings.

The game works the same whatever you choose, and you never have to watch an ad to play or finish it.

5. In-app purchases

Tickets and the monthly pass are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and I never receive your card details, name or email. The app only asks Apple, on your device, whether your Apple Account owns a purchase or an active pass. Apple handles purchase data under Apple's privacy policy and sends me sales reports that do not identify you. You can cancel the monthly pass at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

6. Legal basis (EU and UK users)

  • Personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and measurement: my legitimate interest in funding a free game, balanced against your privacy.
  • Answering your emails: my legitimate interest in helping you.

7. Children

Rail Chronicle is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your iPhone is kept until you delete the app. Emails to me are kept for as long as needed to help you and then deleted, at the latest after 24 months. Google's retention periods are described in Google's privacy policy.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because I don't hold data about you from the app, the quickest way to control it is on your device and through your tracking settings and Google's tools. For anything else, email me and I will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Sweden this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

10. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and its fonts are served from this site rather than a third party. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update it here and change the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting new kinds of data, I will update this policy before that version is released and mention it in the release notes.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: mcederdev@gmail.com
